# lowyr-crypto-regulatory

EU/UK crypto-asset regulatory toolkit: MiCA token classification, white paper review, CASP perimeter mapping, crypto promotions review, token distribution analysis, consultation responses and multi-jurisdiction regulatory research.

Part of the [lowyr](../../README.md) marketplace by Bastien Choquez.

## Install

```
/plugin marketplace add lawgic-ally/lowyr
/plugin install lowyr-crypto-regulatory@lowyr
```

## Skills

| Skill | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `casp-perimeter-mapper` | Map a crypto product or feature set, function by function, onto the ten MiCA crypto-asset services (Art 3(1)(16)) to decide whether CASP authorisation is required, which services must be on the licence, what passporting and reverse-solicitation constraints apply, and which service-specific conduct rules bite (Arts 66–82). |
| `consultation-response-drafter` | Turn a regulator consultation or discussion paper (FCA CP/DP, ESMA/EBA consultation, national competent authority discussion paper, HM Treasury, Commission call for evidence, etc.) into a structured, evidence-backed response in the regulator's own format — question map, position matrix, evidence asks, drafted answers, executive summary and cover letter. |
| `crypto-promotions-checker` | Review crypto marketing — campaigns, social posts, influencer briefs, partner/sponsorship activations (e.g. sports clubs, brands), giveaways, emails, landing pages, app push notifications — against MiCA marketing-communication rules (Arts 7, 29, 53, 66), the UK financial promotions regime, and the frequently missed overlays (prize-draw/gambling, consumer protection, sponsorship and influencer disclosure). |
| `mica-token-classifier` | Classify a token or digital asset under EU MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114) through a fixed, auditable decision tree — out of scope (NFT, financial instrument, other Art 2(4) exclusions), EMT, ART, or "other crypto-asset" (incl. utility token) — then test the Title II offer exemptions. |
| `mica-whitepaper-reviewer` | Review a MiCA crypto-asset white paper (Title II "other crypto-assets", and with adaptations ART/EMT white papers) line by line against Art 6 and Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 and the ITS templates in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984, then output a gap table with severity, the provision breached and a proposed fix. |
| `token-distribution-analyser` | Analyse the legal characterisation of token distribution and engagement mechanics — airdrops, giveaways, quests/points-to-token conversions, staking and holding rewards, loyalty and cashback, burn-to-redeem, referral bonuses, retroactive rewards — across jurisdictions. |
| `web3-regulatory-expert` | Conduct rigorous, source-verified regulatory research on crypto-assets and blockchain technology across any jurisdiction — token issuance/offerings, crypto-asset services (e.g. under MiCA), financial services laws (e.g. MiFID II), payment services laws (e.g. PSD2/PSD3), AML/CFT laws, and smart contracts/DeFi. |

Every skill re-verifies statutory references against primary sources at run time. The verified baseline is in [docs/citation-register.md](../../docs/citation-register.md). Not legal advice; see [DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md).
